CommonShift Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026
Applies to: CommonShift 1.0 offline edition for iOS and Android

Overview

CommonShift Offline is designed to work locally on your device without a CommonShift account or CommonShift server connection. The developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share the schedule information or other content you enter into the app.

Information stored on your device

CommonShift can store your work rotation and shift settings, work jurisdiction and statutory-holiday settings, vacation/on-call/company-day/custom-hours/note entries, People names and schedules, display preferences, and schedule data you import. This information is stored locally in the app's device storage and is not transmitted to CommonShift servers by the offline edition.

Data collection, analytics, advertising, and tracking

CommonShift Offline does not create or require a CommonShift account; upload or synchronize your schedule to CommonShift servers; collect in-app analytics or crash-reporting data; display advertising; use advertising identifiers; track you across apps or websites; or sell or rent personal information.

The production offline configuration disables Expo Updates and cloud configuration. On Android, the production build also explicitly removes the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions. Google Play purchase processing is handled by the Play Store service when the user chooses to buy or restore Pro.

Purchases and CommonShift Pro

CommonShift offers an optional one-time Pro purchase for paid features such as PDF export. Google Play or Apple's App Store processes the payment. CommonShift does not access or store your payment-card or other payment-method details.

The app may receive store product, purchase-state, and ownership information needed to unlock or restore Pro. That entitlement information is used on the device and may be cached locally. CommonShift does not send your purchase record to a CommonShift server in this release.

User-initiated sharing

When you choose to share a schedule code or calendar text, CommonShift passes the content you selected to the operating system's share sheet. CommonShift does not send that content to the developer and does not receive a copy. A third-party app or service you choose from the share sheet handles the content under its own privacy policy and terms.

Imports

Schedule codes that you paste or import are processed locally and stored locally if you choose to save them. The offline edition does not send imported schedule data to CommonShift servers.

Device permissions

CommonShift Offline does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, or device calendars for its normal product flow.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you edit or remove it, clear CommonShift's app storage, reset the device as applicable, or uninstall the app. Your device or operating-system backup service may include app data according to your device and account settings. CommonShift does not receive or control those backups.

Because CommonShift Offline does not maintain a server account or server-side copy of your app data, there is no CommonShift cloud account or cloud data record to request that the developer delete.

Children

CommonShift does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults because the offline edition does not collect user data from the app.

Store and operating-system services

Apple, Google, your device manufacturer, or services you choose to use may process information independently of CommonShift when you download the app, make or restore an optional Pro purchase, use device backups, or share content. Their practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If CommonShift's data practices change in a future version, this policy and the applicable store privacy declarations will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, email chonkbyte.dev@gmail.com or use the developer support contact published on the CommonShift listing in the App Store or Google Play.
